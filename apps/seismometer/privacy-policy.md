<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">👋</span> Introduction</h2>
  <p>Seismometer respects your privacy. It's designed to collect as little as possible: there's no account, no sign-up and nothing you need to upload. This page explains what data is (and isn't) collected, how it's used, and your rights as a user.</p>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">📋</span> Information We Collect</h2>

  <h3>Information You Provide</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>We do not collect any direct identifiers about you personally. There is no sign-up process or login.</li>
    <li>Seismometer does not use your location, photos, microphone or contacts.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Sensor Readings</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>The only sensor Seismometer reads is the accelerometer (motion sensor), which needs no permission. It is read only while a screen that shows live readings is open and the app is in the foreground.</li>
    <li>Sensor readings are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Information Stored on Your Device</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Your recordings, their names and your settings are stored only on your device.</li>
    <li>This data is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the app.</li>
    <li>If you export a recording as an image or CSV file (a Pro feature), the app hands the file to the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it, and we never see it.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Purchases</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Purchases of Seismometer Pro are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.</li>
    <li>RevenueCat manages your purchase status using an anonymous app user ID, so the app knows whether Pro is unlocked and can restore your purchase. This ID is not linked to your name, email address or Apple ID.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Crash Reports and App Analytics</h3>
  <p>If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and app analytics (for example, how many people use the app and on which iOS versions). This information is aggregated and does not personally identify you.</p>

  <h3>Usage Statistics</h3>
  <p>We use Mixpanel, our analytics provider, to count how a few parts of the app are used, so we can improve it. Only these events are recorded:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>the app was opened;</li>
    <li>the introduction was finished;</li>
    <li>a recording was saved, with its length rounded to ten seconds;</li>
    <li>a recording was exported, with the format (image or CSV);</li>
    <li>a lesson was opened, with which lesson it was;</li>
    <li>the Pro screen was shown, with which feature led to it.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>No event carries a recording, sensor data, or any name you typed, such as the name of a recording.</p>
  <p>In the EU, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the app can't tell which region you're in, nothing is sent unless you turn sharing on. Elsewhere, sharing is on by default. The app goes by your device's region setting and doesn't look up where you are. You can turn sharing on or off at any time in the app under <code>Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics</code>, and the app works the same either way.</p>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">🔧</span> How Your Information Is Used</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>To provide the core functionality of Seismometer, including saving your recordings and settings on your device.</li>
    <li>To unlock and restore Seismometer Pro.</li>
    <li>To fix crashes and improve reliability, performance and future features.</li>
    <li>To understand general trends in how the app is used.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="privacy-highlight">
    <p>We do <strong>not</strong> sell or rent your information or share it with advertisers, and Seismometer contains <strong>no ads</strong>. We only use the service providers listed below.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">🗄️</span> Data Retention</h2>
  <p>Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or delete the app. Anonymous purchase status, crash reports and usage statistics are retained by the relevant service only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.</p>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">⚖️</span> Your Rights</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>You can delete the app at any time, which removes all of your Seismometer data from your device.</li>
    <li>You can turn usage statistics on or off at any time in the app: <code>Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics</code>.</li>
    <li>You can opt out of sharing analytics with app developers via your device settings: <code>Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements</code>.</li>
    <li>If you have any questions about your data, or would like us to delete anything held by our service providers, contact us using the details below.</li>
  </ul>
