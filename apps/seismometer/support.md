<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">❓</span> Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
  <div class="faq-list">
    <details>
      <summary>Why is my trace flat?</summary>
      <p>A flat trace usually just means not much is moving the phone. On a quiet, solid surface that's normal: what's left is mostly the phone's own electronic noise. Try tapping the table near the phone, or walking past it.</p>
      <p>If small vibrations still don't show up, tap the sensitivity button beside the trace and choose Auto or High. A soft surface such as a bed or sofa, or a thick case, can soak up vibrations, so a table, floor or worktop works best.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Why did the trace jump when I picked the phone up?</summary>
      <p>Seismometer measures the movement of the phone itself. Picking it up, turning it or nudging it is a big movement compared with the small vibrations the app is looking for, so the trace jumps. Put the phone back down flat and leave it still, and the trace settles again. The app tells you if the phone isn't lying flat and still.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Can Seismometer detect or warn of earthquakes?</summary>
      <p>No. Seismometer is not an earthquake detector or a warning system. A resting phone picks up its own electronic noise, which is far larger than the motion of small or distant earthquakes, so they don't show up. No single phone can warn before shaking starts: early warning needs a network of stations near the fault and a way to alert people before the waves arrive. Always follow official guidance from your local authorities. For information about real earthquakes, try my <a href="/apps/earthquake-tracker/">Earthquake Tracker</a> app.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>How accurate are the readings?</summary>
      <p>They're only as accurate as your phone's accelerometer, which isn't calibrated as a measuring instrument. The app measures the acceleration of the phone, not the ground, and the surface the phone is lying on can soften or amplify the motion beneath it. The smallest readings are the phone's own noise. The readings are for learning and curiosity, and for comparing one vibration with another. Please don't use them for engineering, structural, workplace, legal or safety decisions. The intensity guide is an educational comparison, not an official measurement.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>What's free, and what does Seismometer Pro add?</summary>
      <p><strong>Free:</strong> the live trace (combined, last 10 seconds), amplitude, peak, dominant frequency, the intensity guide, source hints, Meter mode, four sensitivity levels (Auto, High, Medium and Low), 3 saved recordings, the first 2 lessons, and a 6-second preview of the X, Y and Z traces.</p>
      <p><strong>Pro:</strong> separate X, Y and Z traces, frequency analysis (a live spectrum and the spectrum of a recording), unlimited recordings, export as an image or CSV file, all 6 lessons, and advanced sensitivity (a custom scale, with 5 to 60 seconds shown).</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Is Seismometer Pro a subscription?</summary>
      <p>No. Seismometer Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything for good. Nothing renews, so there's nothing to cancel.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>How do I restore my purchase?</summary>
      <p>Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Seismometer Pro. Then go to <code>Settings → Restore purchases</code> in the app. No separate account is needed.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Can I get a refund?</summary>
      <p>Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at <a href="https://reportaproblem.apple.com">reportaproblem.apple.com</a>.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Where are my recordings kept, and how do I export them?</summary>
      <p>Your recordings are stored only on your device, in the Recordings tab. They aren't backed up to us, and they're deleted if you delete the app. The free version keeps 3 recordings, and Pro keeps as many as you like.</p>
      <p>With Pro, open a recording and choose Image or CSV under Export. The app opens the iOS share sheet, so you can save the file or send it wherever you choose.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Does Seismometer work offline?</summary>
      <p>Yes. The trace, readings, recordings and lessons all work without an internet connection. You only need a connection to buy or restore Pro, and to send usage statistics if you've chosen to share them.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Which devices are supported?</summary>
      <p>Seismometer runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later. On iPad it uses the iPhone layout at a larger size.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Which accessibility features are supported?</summary>
      <p>Seismometer follows your device's settings for larger text, Bold Text, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion and VoiceOver, and works in landscape on iPhone:</p>
      <p><strong>Larger text:</strong> <code>Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text</code>.</p>
      <p><strong>Bold Text and Increase Contrast:</strong> <code>Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size</code>.</p>
      <p><strong>Reduce Motion:</strong> <code>Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion</code>.</p>
      <p><strong>VoiceOver:</strong> <code>Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver</code>.</p>
      <p>The app's own Settings also has Increase contrast and Reduce motion switches, if you want them in this app only. Read the <a href="/apps/seismometer/accessibility/">Accessibility</a> page to see how the app works with each setting, and what hasn't been checked yet.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>How do I change units?</summary>
      <p>Go to <code>Settings → Units</code> in the app and choose m/s² or g.</p>
    </details>
