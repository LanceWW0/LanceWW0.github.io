  <p>The App reads your device's accelerometer and shows what it measures as a seismograph trace, with readings and explanations. You acknowledge that:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>The App is not an earthquake detector or a warning system. A phone cannot detect small or distant earthquakes and cannot warn before shaking starts.</li>
    <li>The App measures the acceleration of the device itself, not the motion of the ground. A device resting on a surface records that surface, which may soften or amplify the motion of the floor, building and ground beneath it.</li>
    <li>Readings come from a phone sensor that is not calibrated as a measuring instrument. They are provided for learning and curiosity only.</li>
    <li>You must not use the App's readings for engineering, structural, workplace, legal or safety decisions.</li>
    <li>The intensity guide is an educational comparison, not an official measurement of shaking or earthquake intensity. Source hints are suggestions and may be wrong.</li>
    <li>In an earthquake, you should always follow official guidance from your local authorities and emergency services.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="privacy-warning">
