<section class="privacy-section" aria-labelledby="introduction">
  <h2 id="introduction"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true">👋</span> Introduction</h2>
  <p>Volcano Lab works on iPhone and iPad. It follows the accessibility settings you choose in Settings &gt; Accessibility, so there's nothing to set up inside the app.</p>
  <p>The app has no spoken audio, so nothing depends on sound.</p>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section" aria-labelledby="larger-text">
  <h2 id="larger-text"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true">🔠</span> Larger Text</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Every screen works up to the largest accessibility text size.</li>
    <li>At the largest sizes, words written over the 3D eruption move underneath it.</li>
    <li>In the first-run tour, the words sit in a panel that scrolls. Each caption, and the card about who made the app, waits until you tap Continue.</li>
    <li>Wide equations in "Behind the model" scroll sideways.</li>
    <li>Touch and hold a tab to see it larger.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section" aria-labelledby="dark-mode">
  <h2 id="dark-mode"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true">🌙</span> Dark Mode</h2>
  <p>Every screen follows your device's Dark Mode setting. The first-run tour is always dark, because it's a night scene.</p>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section" aria-labelledby="colour">
  <h2 id="colour"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true">🎨</span> Colour</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Nothing relies on colour alone.</li>
    <li>With Differentiate Without Colour switched on, the selected eruption preset also shows a checkmark.</li>
    <li>The pyroclastic flow warning has a symbol and words, not just a colour.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section" aria-labelledby="contrast">
  <h2 id="contrast"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true">🌓</span> Contrast</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, and controls at least 3:1. This holds in light mode, in dark mode and with Increase Contrast switched on.</li>
    <li>Words over the 3D eruption sit on a dark backing, so they stay readable.</li>
    <li>With Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast switched on, see-through panels become solid.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section" aria-labelledby="reduce-motion">
  <h2 id="reduce-motion"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true">⏸️</span> Reduce Motion</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>The camera stops turning by itself and doesn't shake, and the stars stop twinkling.</li>
    <li>The cutaway, the video on the Pro screen and changes between screens respond to the setting too.</li>
    <li>In the first-run tour, the camera stays still.</li>
    <li>The eruption itself still moves. The tour, the Lab and the lessons each have a pause button for it.</li>
    <li>While the eruption is paused, you can still move the sliders. The picture catches up when you press play.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section" aria-labelledby="smart-invert">
  <h2 id="smart-invert"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true">🔄</span> Smart Invert</h2>
  <p>With Smart Invert switched on, the eruption, maps, photos and video keep their true colours.</p>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section" aria-labelledby="voiceover">
  <h2 id="voiceover"><span class="section-icon" aria-hidden="true">🗣️</span> VoiceOver, Switch Control and Voice Control</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Every button and slider has a label.</li>
    <li>The 3D eruption is described in words, including what kind of eruption it is and when pyroclastic flows are running.</li>
    <li>With VoiceOver, you can rotate, tilt and zoom the view using actions instead of gestures.</li>
    <li>With VoiceOver or Switch Control switched on, the first-run tour waits for you instead of moving on by itself.</li>
    <li>Every control has a name you can say with Voice Control. The camera has buttons too: say "Tap Move the view", then "Tap Rotate left" or "Tap Zoom in".</li>
  </ul>
</section>
