<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">👋</span> Introduction</h2>
  <p>Volcano Lab respects your privacy. It's designed to collect as little as possible: there's no account, no sign-up and nothing you need to upload. This page explains what data is (and isn't) collected, how it's used, and your rights as a user.</p>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">📋</span> Information We Collect</h2>

  <h3>Information You Provide</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>We do not collect any direct identifiers about you personally. There is no sign-up process or login.</li>
    <li>Volcano Lab does not ask for, upload or store photos, and it does not track your location.</li>
    <li>Volcano Lab does not use AI or OpenAI services to process anything you do in the app.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Information Stored on Your Device</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Your preferences, saved lab runs, favourites and challenge progress are stored only on your device.</li>
    <li>This data is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the app.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Purchases</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Purchases of Volcano Lab Pro are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.</li>
    <li>RevenueCat manages your purchase status using an anonymous app user ID, so the app knows whether Pro is unlocked and can restore your purchase. This ID is not linked to your name, email address or Apple ID.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Crash Reports and App Analytics</h3>
  <p>If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and app analytics (for example, how many people use the app and on which iOS versions). This information is aggregated and does not personally identify you.</p>

  <!-- TODO: confirm Mixpanel. Keep this sub-section if Mixpanel is added to the app; delete it (and the Mixpanel item under Third-Party Services) if not. -->
  <h3>Product Analytics</h3>
  <p>We may use Mixpanel to collect anonymous usage events, such as which features are opened or which challenges are completed, to understand how the app is used and improve it. These events are not linked to your name, email address or any other personal identifier.</p>

  <!-- TODO: confirm the live volcano activity feed. Keep this sub-section if the feature ships; delete it if not. -->
  <h3>Live Volcano Activity</h3>
  <p>Some features may show live volcano activity fetched from public sources such as the <a href="https://volcano.si.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program</a> and the <a href="https://www.usgs.gov/programs/VHP" target="_blank" rel="noopener">USGS Volcano Hazards Program</a>. Fetching this data involves a standard network request (which, like any request on the internet, includes your device's IP address), but no personal data is sent beyond that.</p>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">🔧</span> How Your Information Is Used</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>To provide the core functionality of Volcano Lab, including saving your lab runs, favourites and progress on your device.</li>
    <li>To unlock and restore Volcano Lab Pro.</li>
    <li>To fix crashes and improve reliability, performance and future features.</li>
    <li>To understand general trends in how the app is used.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="privacy-highlight">
    <p>We do <strong>not</strong> sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or third parties, and Volcano Lab contains <strong>no ads</strong>.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">🗄️</span> Data Retention</h2>
  <p>Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or delete the app. Anonymous purchase status, crash reports and analytics are retained by the relevant service only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.</p>
</section>

  <ul>
    <li>You can delete the app at any time, which removes all of your Volcano Lab data from your device.</li>
    <li>You can opt out of sharing analytics with app developers via your device settings: <code>Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements</code>.</li>
