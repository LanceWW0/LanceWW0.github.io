<section class="privacy-section">
  <h2><span class="section-icon">❓</span> Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
  <div class="faq-list">
    <details>
      <summary>Why didn't my volcano erupt the way I expected?</summary>
      <p>The style of an eruption mostly comes down to two things: how sticky (viscous) the magma is, and how much gas it holds. Each of the four controls in the Eruption Lab changes one or both:</p>
      <p><strong>Silica (SiO<sub>2</sub>):</strong> more silica makes the magma stickier.</p>
      <p><strong>Temperature:</strong> cooler magma is stickier.</p>
      <p><strong>Crystal content:</strong> more crystals make the magma stiffer and can clog the conduit.</p>
      <p><strong>Dissolved water:</strong> more water means more gas to drive the eruption.</p>
      <p>Hot, runny, low-silica magma tends to give gentle Hawaiian or Strombolian eruptions. Sticky, gas-rich magma traps its gas until it bursts out explosively, giving Vulcanian, Sub-Plinian or Plinian eruptions. After every eruption, the app explains in plain English why it behaved the way it did.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>What's free, and what does Volcano Lab Pro add?</summary>
      <p><strong>Free:</strong> the 3D Eruption Lab with all four magma controls, the Kīlauea and Stromboli presets, the Explore map of volcanoes, the first lessons and the first challenges.</p>
      <p><strong>Pro:</strong> the cutaway view, the famous eruptions (Mount St Helens 1980, Vesuvius 79 AD and Pinatubo 1991), the advanced controls (vent radius and eruption rate), and all lessons and challenges.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Is Volcano Lab Pro a subscription?</summary>
      <p>No. Volcano Lab Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything for good. Nothing renews, so there's nothing to cancel.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>How do I restore my purchase?</summary>
      <p>Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Volcano Lab Pro. Then tap Restore purchases in the app. No separate account is needed.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Can I get a refund?</summary>
      <p>Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at <a href="https://reportaproblem.apple.com">reportaproblem.apple.com</a>.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Does Volcano Lab work offline?</summary>
      <p>Yes. Once it's installed, the Eruption Lab, lessons and volcano profiles all work without an internet connection.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Can Volcano Lab predict real eruptions?</summary>
      <p>No. Volcano Lab is an educational simulator. Its models are simplified for learning and don't use live monitoring data, so it can't tell you when or how a real volcano will erupt. Always follow official guidance from your local volcano observatory and civil authorities.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Which accessibility features are supported?</summary>
      <p>Volcano Lab supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion, using your device's settings:</p>
      <p><strong>Larger text:</strong> <code>Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text</code>.</p>
      <p><strong>VoiceOver:</strong> <code>Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver</code>.</p>
      <p><strong>Reduce Motion:</strong> <code>Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion</code>.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Can I use Volcano Lab in my classroom?</summary>
      <p>Yes, please do! Volcano Lab was designed as a learning tool. If you're a teacher and have questions or ideas, get in touch using the details below.</p>
    </details>
